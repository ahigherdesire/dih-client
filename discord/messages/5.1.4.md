## DIH Client 5.1.4 is out
Fabric, NeoForge and Forge on Minecraft 26.2 and 26.3; Fabric on 1.21.11 and 26.1 (26.1.2).
**Download: https://dih.lxysg.com**
Stable release. 5.1.4 is now the main purple download button.

**New: Storage Search**
- `.storage search iron ingot` finds items in containers you've opened and highlights matching storage in green. Search by item name or ID, including names with spaces.
- Results show item counts, coordinates, distance and when you last saw the contents. Open and close storage to refresh its saved contents.
- Highlights show up to 64 containers within 128 blocks in your current dimension. `.storage clear` hides them without deleting memory.
- `#chest` searches use the same memory. `#chest 1 highlight` selects one result. The old `goto` syntax now highlights too; it won't walk you there.

**Fixed**
- Container recording now catches normal storage screens, including sessions longer than eight seconds.
- Emptying a container removes its stale item records. Opening either half of a double chest no longer duplicates it.
- Unrelated menus aren't recorded as chests. Storage memory stays separate between worlds and servers.

To update, replace your old DIH jar in `mods` with the 5.1.4 jar for your Minecraft version and loader. Found a bug or want something new? Press **Report Bug** or **Request Feature** below.
